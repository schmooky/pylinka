Brackeys VFX Bundle — texture credits
=====================================

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 / public domain). No attribution is
required, but credit is given here because these creators deserve it.

Particle textures ... Picster  (https://github.com/RPicster/Godot-particle-and-vfx-textures)
                      Kenney   (https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack)
Flipbooks .......... Thomas Iché (https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks)
Pre-drawn sheets ... CodeManu (https://codemanu.itch.io/vfx-free-pack)

Repackaged (and some modified) by Brackeys. For this repo the images were
downscaled and re-sliced to exact grid multiples so the atlas UVs stay clean:
  opaque/  single 128×128 sprites, additive-friendly (sit on black)
  alpha/   single 128×128 sprites with real transparency (normal blend)
  sheets/  animated flipbooks (8×8) and pre-drawn grids (cols×rows in the name)
